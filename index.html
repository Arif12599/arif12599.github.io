01 — About

Engineer by trade, problem-solver by instinct

I've spent my career in the space where IT meets the factory floor — where a firewall rule, a PLC tag and a production target are all part of the same conversation.

As IT Support Engineer at the Cummins COE Plant in Phaltan, I've owned everything from OT network troubleshooting and MES application support to digital transformation initiatives that changed how the plant works day to day.

My favourite kind of problem is the one nobody can categorise — the AMR that won't talk to the PLC, the MES transaction stuck between ERP and the line, the VLAN that works everywhere except the one place it matters. I enjoy pulling the right teams together, diagnosing methodically, and closing it properly rather than temporarily.

Alongside that, I've built Power Apps for HSE and maintenance checksheets, supported the VMS visitor portal rollout, driven EMS/BMS and OT rack infrastructure projects, and supported plant-wide AI and application rebranding rollouts — always with the end user in mind.

None of it happened alone. I owe a great deal to my manager and the wider IT Leadership team at Cummins — for the trust, the guidance, and the freedom to take ownership of problems well beyond my job description.

Now I'm carrying that blend of manufacturing depth and modern DevOps/cloud practice into my next role.
